Adhyay 1

Akela Bachpan

Ek ladka tha, Sandeep Kumar Meena. Pyaar se log use Sachin kehte the. Wo bachpan se achha tha, sach bolta tha, sabki baat sunta tha.

Phir bhi ghar mein usse aur uske chhote bhai ke beech farak kiya jaata. Wo hamesha akela raha. Akelapan, udaasi, kam bol pana aur kamzor sharir, ye sab uske saath badhte gaye.

Adhyay 2

Do Bhai, Do Nazariye

Chhote bhai se ghar kuch nahi kehta tha, kyunki wo sabki maan leta tha. Sandeep sach bolta tha, isliye sab usse galat samajhte the.

Uske liye sirf dhamki thi aur warning. "Top karna hai, bas." Hausla badhane wala koi nahi tha.

Adhyay 3 · 2022-23

10th: Bina Sahare Ki Jeet

81%

Class 10 mein koi use motivate nahi karta tha. Phir bhi 2023 mein usne poore school mein 15th rank li, aur apne section aur class mein top kiya, 81% ke saath.

Par ghar wale tab bhi naraz rahe. Us din Sandeep ne ghar se umeed rakhna chhod diya.

Adhyay 4

Ek Khilona Jaisa

11th mein usse zabardasti Maths-Science dilaya gaya. Usne wahi padha aur pehle class exam mein top kiya. Par ghar mein kisi ne yakeen nahi kiya.

Phir wo average ho gaya, bas padhta tha. Fir bhi sab kehte, "Ye to game khelta rehta hai." Sunday ko bahar khelna bhi band ho gaya.

Tab usse laga, jaise wo duniya mein ek toy hai, jise sab apni marzi se chalate hain.

Adhyay 5

Apne Raaste Par

Usne ghar se baat karna bahut kam kar diya aur JEE Mains aur Advanced ki taiyari mein lag gaya. Par ghar walon ne use phir school mein daal diya, "12th mein top karna hai."

Is baar usne apne tareeke se padha. 12th mein 77.66% aaye. Phir JEE Mains ke do exam diye, aur dono mein nahi nikla. Ek saal ki mehnat poori nahi baithi.

Adhyay 6

Garam Phone, Tootta Hausla

Phir ek saal online classes ka mauka mila. Par uska phone purana tha, sirf 3.5 GB ka, aur bahut garam ho jaata tha. Ek mahine baad hi padhai ruk gayi.

Usne ghar mein sabko bataya, madad maangi. Par kisi ne kuch nahi kiya. Us din usne sab se umeed chhod di.

Adhyay 7

Train Ka Safar

Stage 1 nikal gaya, par Stage 2 bahut mushkil tha, aur wo nahi nikal paya. Ghar wale bahut naraz the.

Exam dekar jab wo train se ghar laut raha tha, uska dil bahut bhaari tha. Phone mein koi dost nahi tha jisse wo apni baat keh sake.

Adhyay 8

Tian Tian

Hello Talk, ek language learning app par uski mulakat hui Tian Tian se. Wo China se thi, aur Sandeep India se.

Us waqt Sandeep bahut udaas tha. Usne apni saari baatein Tian Tian se kahi. Tian Tian ne pyaar se suna, uska dard samjha. Pehli baar dil ki baat keh kar use bahut achha laga.

Adhyay 9

"I Love You"

Sandeep ko Tian Tian se pyaar ho gaya. Usne propose kar diya, "I love you." Tian Tian pehle bahut shock hui.

Phir boli, "Hum ek-doosre ko time dete hain, aage dekhte hain." Sandeep maan gaya, aur aaj tak dono ek-doosre ke saath khush hain.

Adhyay 10

Jo Bhej Nahi Saka, Wo Bana Diya

Rupaya kamzor aur yuan mazboot, isliye Sandeep kabhi koi physical gift nahi bhej paya. Ye baat use hamesha khalegi.

Par usne Tian Tian ke birthday ke liye khud websites banayi. Unhe dekhkar wo bahut khush hui. Sachche tohfe wahi hote hain jo dil se bane hon.

Adhyay 11

Sapne Aur Sawaal

Sandeep ne tay kiya hai: 4 saal ki B.Tech, phir M.Tech, taaki job ke chances badhein aur wo Tian Tian ke saath kahin reh sake. College ke baad wo use milne jayega.

Kabhi dar bhi lagta hai. Kya Tian Tian chhod degi? Hum ek-doosre ki bhasha kaise samjhenge? Kya dono ke ghar wale apnayenge?

In sawalon ke jawab waqt dega. Aaj itna pata hai: dono roz baat karte hain, aur ek-doosre ko seekh rahe hain.

Kahani Abhi Baaki Hai

Jisne akele rehkar bhi haar nahi maani, uske liye raaste zaroor banenge. Ab uske saath sabse bada saathi hai, uska apna hausla.
